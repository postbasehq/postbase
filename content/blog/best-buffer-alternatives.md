Buffer is a good scheduler. It's simple, it has a real free plan, and it covers more networks than most. People usually go looking for an alternative for one of three reasons:

- **Per-channel pricing.** Buffer's Essentials plan is $5 per channel per month. That's cheap for three accounts and less so for fifteen.
- **They want something specific:** threads-first writing, a shared inbox, open source, or an AI agent that schedules for them.
- **They're a developer** and want an API or MCP server at a price that suits a side project.

This list covers the alternatives worth a look, with an honest "best for" for each. Prices and features are from each product's official pages, checked on 26–27 September 2026. Plans change, so check before you buy.

## 1. Postbase: best for AI agents and flat pricing

That's us, so weigh this one accordingly. Postbase schedules to X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon, TikTok and YouTube from one calendar, with a version of each post per network. It has an AI agent built in, and a hosted MCP server, so Claude, Cursor and other AI tools can schedule posts for you.

- **Price:** $29/month for 5 channels; $39/month for 15 channels and team seats.
- **vs Buffer:** cheaper from about six channels up, open source, and built for AI agents.
- **Not yet:** Instagram, Facebook and Threads (waiting on Meta's review), and no free plan beyond the trial.

[Postbase vs Buffer →](/alternatives/buffer)

## 2. Postiz: best open-source option with the most networks

Postiz is open source (AGPL-3.0), self-hostable, and supports more than 30 networks, including Reddit, Discord, Telegram and Pinterest. Its MCP server, CLI and API are on every plan.

- **Price:** $29/month for 5 channels, up to $99/month for 100.
- **vs Buffer:** far more networks and self-hosting; no free hosted plan, and no team seats on the cheapest plan.

## 3. Publer: best for the lowest price on a few accounts

Publer is the budget pick, with a genuine free plan and per-account pricing from $5. It also has one of the longest network lists here, including Mastodon, Bluesky, Telegram and WordPress.

- **Price:** from $5 per account per month; free plan for 3 accounts (excluding X).
- **vs Buffer:** similar pricing model with more networks; the API and MCP server are only on Business plans.

[Postbase vs Publer →](/alternatives/publer)

## 4. SocialBee: best for evergreen content recycling

SocialBee is built around content categories that refill your queue automatically, which suits small businesses that post a steady mix of evergreen content.

- **Price:** $29/month for 5 profiles; extra users $10/month each.
- **vs Buffer:** stronger recycling; no public API or MCP server, and no Mastodon.

[Postbase vs SocialBee →](/alternatives/socialbee)

## 5. Post Bridge: best for high-volume short-form video

Post Bridge is a simple, high-volume scheduler popular with short-form video accounts, with an API and MCP server on its current plans.

- **Price:** $39/month for 15 accounts.
- **vs Buffer:** flat pricing at volume and bulk video scheduling; no Mastodon, lighter analytics.

[Postbase vs Post Bridge →](/alternatives/post-bridge)

## 6. Typefully: best for writing threads on X and LinkedIn

Typefully is the most polished writing tool here, with a great thread editor and X growth tools. Its API and MCP server are on every plan, including Free.

- **Price:** free plan for one account; paid plans billed yearly.
- **vs Buffer:** far better for writers; text networks only (no TikTok, YouTube or Instagram).

[Postbase vs Typefully →](/alternatives/typefully)

## 7. Later: best for Instagram and TikTok-first brands

Later is a visual planner built around Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest, with an influencer marketing product alongside.

- **Price:** $25/month (Starter), with a 30-posts-per-profile monthly cap.
- **vs Buffer:** better for visual planning; no X, Bluesky or Mastodon, and no public posting API.

[Postbase vs Later →](/alternatives/later)

## 8. Hootsuite: best for larger teams that need an inbox and listening

Hootsuite is a full suite: publishing, a shared inbox, social listening and reporting, plus MCP servers for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot.

- **Price:** $99 per user per month, billed yearly.
- **vs Buffer:** much more capability, at a much higher price.

[Postbase vs Hootsuite →](/alternatives/hootsuite)

## 9. Sprout Social: best for enterprise reporting

Sprout Social is the enterprise pick, with a deep unified inbox, listening and reporting. Its API is only on the top tier, and its MCP server is documented for ChatGPT.

- **Price:** $99 per seat per month ($79 billed yearly).
- **vs Buffer:** far deeper for large teams, and priced for them.

[Postbase vs Sprout Social →](/alternatives/sprout-social)

## When to stay with Buffer

Buffer is still the right pick if you post to two or three channels, want a free plan, need Instagram or Pinterest today, and don't need an AI agent or API. Switching has a cost, even when it's small.

## How to choose

- **Mostly text, mostly X?** Typefully.
- **Want open source?** Postbase or Postiz.
- **Lowest price for a few accounts?** Publer, or stay on Buffer.
- **Lots of accounts at a flat price?** Postbase, Post Bridge or SocialBee.
- **Visual, Instagram-first?** Later.
- **Big team with inbox and listening needs?** Hootsuite or Sprout Social.
- **Want Claude or ChatGPT to post for you?** Postbase, Buffer, Typefully, Postiz and Post Bridge all have MCP servers. See [the best MCP servers for social media](/blog/best-mcp-servers-for-social-media).
