Hootsuite is a serious tool: publishing, a shared inbox, social listening, reporting, and MCP servers for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot. It's also priced like one. Its Standard plan is **$99 per user per month, billed yearly**, and API access needs an application.

Most people looking for an alternative mainly need scheduling and a calendar, sometimes with a light inbox. If that's you, these options cost far less. Prices are from each product's official pages, checked on 26–27 September 2026.

## If you need the full suite: Sprout Social

Sprout Social is the closest like-for-like replacement, with a deep unified inbox, listening and reporting. It isn't cheaper per seat ($99, or $79 billed yearly), and its API is only on the top tier, but some teams prefer its reporting and interface.

## If you mainly need scheduling

### Buffer

Simple, well-loved, with a real free plan and per-channel pricing. Great for two or three channels; the cost climbs as you add accounts.

### Postbase

That's us, so weigh this one accordingly. A calendar for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon, TikTok and YouTube, an AI agent built in, and an MCP server so Claude and other AI tools can schedule posts. Team is $39/month for 15 channels with team seats, and each client can have their own workspace. Instagram, Facebook and Threads are waiting on Meta's review, and there's no inbox or listening.

[Postbase vs Hootsuite →](/alternatives/hootsuite)

### Postiz

Open source, 30+ networks, and an API, CLI and MCP server on every plan. The widest coverage at a Postbase-like price.

### Publer

The budget pick: a free plan and per-account pricing from $5, with a long network list including Mastodon and WordPress. The API is on Business plans only.

[Postbase vs Publer →](/alternatives/publer)

### SocialBee

Built around content categories that keep your queue topped up with evergreen posts. No public API.

[Postbase vs SocialBee →](/alternatives/socialbee)

### Later

A visual planner for Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest. No X, Bluesky or Mastodon.

[Postbase vs Later →](/alternatives/later)

### Post Bridge

Flat pricing for lots of accounts ($39/month for 15) and bulk video scheduling, with an API and MCP server included.

## When Hootsuite is still the right call

Stay with Hootsuite (or move to Sprout) if your team lives in the shared inbox, relies on social listening or competitor benchmarking, or needs enterprise approvals and a support contract. None of the cheaper tools here replace those well.

## How to switch without losing anything

1. **Run both for a week.** Most alternatives have a 7–14 day trial.
2. **Connect the same accounts** in the new tool. Connecting doesn't disconnect them from Hootsuite.
3. **Move your queue:** recreate scheduled posts, then delete them in Hootsuite so nothing posts twice.
4. **Export your reports** from Hootsuite before you cancel. Analytics history usually doesn't transfer.
