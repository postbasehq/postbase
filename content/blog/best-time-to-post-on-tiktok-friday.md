**The best time to post on TikTok on Friday is between 3pm and 6pm in your audience's local time.** It's the rare window where all three large studies agree: Sprout Social says 3pm to 5pm, Hootsuite 4pm to 6pm, and Buffer's best Friday slot is 6pm. For creators, Friday evening up to 10pm is a good second window.

Friday is one of the more reliable days on TikTok, for brands and creators alike. Here's the detail, checked on 10 November 2026.

## What the studies say about Fridays

| Study | Best Friday times | How Friday ranks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-TikTok/) (2026, nearly 2 billion engagements) | 3pm to 5pm | High engagement, just behind Tuesday to Thursday |
| [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/) (2025, over 1 million posts) | 4pm to 6pm | A solid afternoon day |
| [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) (2026, 7.1 million posts) | 6pm, then 10pm and 8pm | Evenings are strongest |

Line them up and the overlap is clear: **late Friday afternoon, from about 3pm to 6pm**. Brand accounts peak at the start of that window; creator accounts at the end of it and into the evening.

## Best Friday times by industry

Sprout Social's industry breakdown puts nearly every industry in the late afternoon on Fridays, with software and tech the outlier:

| Industry | Best Friday time |
| --- | --- |
| Retail | 12pm, and 2pm to 4pm |
| Food and drink | 2pm to 5pm |
| Healthcare | 4pm to 6pm |
| Travel and hospitality | 4pm and 6pm |
| Education | 5pm |
| Government | 5pm to 6pm |
| Financial services | 3pm, 5pm to 6pm, and 11pm |
| Nonprofits | 4pm to 10pm |
| Software and tech | 7am to 9am |

Tech audiences check TikTok before work on a Friday, then switch off. Everyone else picks up as the working week winds down.

## Why Friday afternoons work

By mid-afternoon on a Friday, attention is drifting from work to the weekend. School is out, people are commuting home early, and phones come out. That's the window brand accounts hit hardest. As the evening goes on, creators' audiences take over: Buffer's data has Friday's strong hours at 6pm, 8pm and 10pm, when people are home, out, or winding down.

Friday mornings are weaker for most accounts. Unless you're in tech, a 9am Friday post gives up the best part of the day.

## Friday times in EST, CST and PST

The studies use your viewers' local time. For a national US audience, aim at Eastern time and check what that means further west:

| Eastern (ET) | Central (CT) | Mountain (MT) | Pacific (PT) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3pm | 2pm | 1pm | 12pm |
| 5pm | 4pm | 3pm | 2pm |
| 6pm | 5pm | 4pm | 3pm |

**6pm Eastern suits the whole US well**: it's the end of the window in the East and 3pm on the West Coast, right inside Sprout's afternoon window there too.

## Friday night: worth it?

For creators, yes. Buffer ranks 8pm and 10pm among Friday's best hours, and Sprout's nonprofit data runs to 10pm. For businesses, Friday night is mostly wasted: post in the afternoon and let the video carry into the evening on its own.

## Find your own best Friday time

Open **TikTok Studio**, go to **Analytics**, then **Followers**, and check **Follower activity** for Friday. Note the time zone shown, which may be UTC. Then test two Friday slots, say 3pm and 6pm, with similar videos over a few weeks, and keep the one with more views at 24 hours.

## Schedule Friday's video in advance

Friday afternoon is when a lot of people are wrapping up work, which makes it an easy slot to miss. Schedule it earlier in the week: TikTok Studio on a desktop lets you schedule up to 10 days ahead ([here's how](/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-videos)), or use a tool like Postbase to keep TikTok on one calendar with YouTube and your other networks. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best time to post on TikTok on Friday?

Between 3pm and 6pm in your audience's local time. Sprout Social, Hootsuite and Buffer all put Friday's best times inside or at the edge of that window.

### Is Friday a good day to post on TikTok?

Yes. Sprout Social rates Friday afternoon as high engagement, and it's one of the few days where the big studies agree on the best hours.

### Is it good to post on TikTok on Friday night?

For creators, it can be: Buffer ranks 8pm and 10pm among Friday's strongest hours. For brands, post in the afternoon instead.

### What is the best time to post on TikTok on Friday in EST?

3pm to 6pm Eastern. For a national US audience, 6pm Eastern is a good choice, as it's 3pm on the West Coast.

### Should I post on TikTok on Friday morning?

Only if your audience is in tech or business, where Sprout's data shows 7am to 9am working best. For most accounts, the afternoon is much stronger.
