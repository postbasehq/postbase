**The best time to post on TikTok on Saturday is between 3pm and 6pm in your audience's local time.** Buffer's 2026 study of 7.1 million posts ranked Saturday the best day of the week, with 5pm, 4pm and 3pm its strongest hours, and Hootsuite's data shows a long Saturday window from 10am to 7pm. Brand accounts see the opposite: Sprout Social's data puts weekends at the bottom.

For creators and consumer brands, Saturday is one of the best days to post. Here's the detail, checked on 10 November 2026.

## What the studies say about Saturdays

| Study | Best Saturday times | How Saturday ranks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) (2026, 7.1 million posts) | 5pm, then 4pm and 3pm | The best day of the week |
| [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/) (2025, over 1 million posts) | 10am to 7pm | One of its two best windows of the week |
| [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-TikTok/) (2026, nearly 2 billion engagements) | Avoid for most industries | Low engagement for brand accounts |

Buffer's and Hootsuite's windows overlap from **3pm to 6pm**, which is where to start. Hootsuite's wider window means late morning and midday Saturday posts can work too.

## Best Saturday times by industry

Most industries in Sprout Social's data should skip Saturday. A few shouldn't:

| Industry | Best Saturday time |
| --- | --- |
| Software and tech | 8am to 10am |
| Nonprofits | 11am to 2pm |
| Travel and hospitality | 5pm |
| Financial services | 6pm |
| Retail, food and drink, education, healthcare, government | Weekends are the weakest days |

## Why the studies split on Saturday

Who's being measured makes the difference. Buffer's data includes many creators and small businesses and ranks posts by median engagement rate: how well a typical post does relative to how many people saw it. Fewer accounts post on Saturdays, so each post competes with less, and creator audiences have free time to watch, comment and share. Sprout Social measures brand accounts, whose audiences mostly engage with them during the working week.

So if you're a creator, or a brand people follow for fun (food, fashion, travel, entertainment), Saturday is worth prioritising. If your followers know you from work, it isn't.

## Saturday times in EST, CST and PST

The studies use your viewers' local time. For a US-wide audience, aim at Eastern time and check the West Coast:

| Eastern (ET) | Central (CT) | Mountain (MT) | Pacific (PT) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3pm | 2pm | 1pm | 12pm |
| 5pm | 4pm | 3pm | 2pm |
| 6pm | 5pm | 4pm | 3pm |

**6pm Eastern works across the whole US**: the end of the window in the East and 3pm, the start of it, on the West Coast.

## Saturday morning or Saturday night?

**Morning:** fine for tech audiences (8am to 10am in Sprout's data) and inside Hootsuite's 10am-onwards window, but Buffer's data doesn't favour it. Start later if you can.

**Night:** Saturday evenings after 7pm aren't strong in any of the studies, perhaps because people are out. Post in the afternoon and let the video carry into the evening.

## Find your own best Saturday time

In **TikTok Studio**, go to **Analytics**, then **Followers**, and check **Follower activity** for Saturday. The chart may show UTC rather than local time, so check the label. Weekend patterns vary a lot between audiences; students, parents and shift workers all use weekends differently. Test two slots, such as 12pm and 5pm, over a few Saturdays and keep the better one.

## Schedule Saturday's video during the week

A Saturday slot shouldn't cost you your Saturday. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best time to post on TikTok on Saturday?

Between 3pm and 6pm in your audience's local time. Buffer's best Saturday hours are 5pm, 4pm and 3pm, and Hootsuite's Saturday window runs from 10am to 7pm.

### Is Saturday a good day to post on TikTok?

For creators and consumer brands, it's one of the best: Buffer's 2026 data ranks Saturday the top day of the week. For B2B and most brand accounts, Sprout Social's data says to avoid weekends.

### What is the best time to post on TikTok on Saturday in EST?

3pm to 6pm Eastern. For an audience across the US, 6pm Eastern is 3pm on the West Coast, so both coasts are inside the window.

### Should I post on TikTok on Saturday morning?

It can work, especially for tech audiences between 8am and 10am, and Hootsuite's window starts at 10am. But the afternoon is stronger in most of the data.

### Is it bad to post on TikTok on Saturday night?

None of the studies rank Saturday evenings highly. Post in the afternoon instead, unless your own follower activity in TikTok Studio says otherwise.
