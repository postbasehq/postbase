For small agencies, shortlist **SocialPilot for client approvals and branded reports, Agorapulse for community management, and Postbase for scheduling with separate client workspaces and AI tools**. Buffer, Sendible, Metricool and Planable are worth considering when channel-based billing, client onboarding, reporting or approval controls drive the decision. The best Hootsuite alternative is the one that covers your clients' actual networks and review process at the total price you can sustain.

That's us in the list: Postbase publishes this guide. These recommendations are based on public product documentation checked on **November 1, 2026**, rather than hands-on testing of every product. Prices and plan boundaries can change; the linked vendor pages are the place to confirm your quote.

## When is Postbase a good fit?

Postbase suits a publishing-focused agency whose clients use its supported networks and whose sign-off process can happen outside the scheduler. Its [agency page](/for/agencies) describes separate channels, media, calendars and teams for each client workspace.

The [published monthly plans](/pricing) include:

| Plan | Monthly price | Channels | People | Workspaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Team | $39 | 15 | 5 | 3 |
| Pro | $59 | 50 | 15 | 5 |
| Agency | $99 | 100 | 30 | 20 |

Channels and people are shared across the plan's workspaces. Pending invitations count toward the people limit; one person in several workspaces counts once. X posts containing links have monthly allowances: 30 on Team, 45 on Pro and 75 on Agency. Check those limits if client campaigns drive traffic to landing pages.

Postbase includes an MCP server and API, so scheduling can be part of an AI-assisted workflow. It is also open source and can be self-hosted with your own platform keys. See the [Hootsuite comparison](/alternatives/hootsuite) for those capabilities and the distinction between publishing and a full social suite.

The reasons to choose another tool are concrete:

- **Client sign-off:** there is [no built-in client approval workflow yet](/for/agencies). Review drafts separately, then schedule them.
- **Network coverage:** Instagram, Facebook and Threads are listed as coming soon. Supported networks are X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon, TikTok and YouTube, with [TikTok publishing private until public posting is approved](/integrations/tiktok).
- **Community management:** Postbase [does not have a shared social inbox or social listening](/alternatives/hootsuite).

For a LinkedIn-focused agency with an established review process, it belongs on the shortlist. For an Instagram-heavy agency requiring enforced client approval, those gaps are reasons to choose elsewhere today.

## Which alternatives handle client approvals and reports?

### SocialPilot: a practical shortlist for agency publishing

[SocialPilot Premium](https://www.socialpilot.co/plans) lists 25 social accounts, six users, client approval and white-label reports for $100 per month on monthly billing, or $85 per month billed annually. Standard includes 15 accounts and three users, but its approval feature is manager approval.

That distinction matters: an internal manager checking a draft is not necessarily the same workflow as an external client signing it off. During the trial, invite a real reviewer, request a revision and confirm who can publish afterward. Check added-account and added-user charges before budgeting for growth.

### Sendible: consider it when many people collaborate

[Sendible's plans](https://www.sendible.com/pricing) include unlimited users and are organized around workspace allowances. Plus lists three workspaces and assignment and approval features; Premium lists seven workspaces and custom branded reports. White labeling is listed as an add-on under Elite.

This makes Sendible worth pricing when staff, freelancers and client stakeholders all need access. Ask for the quote that covers your workspace and profile counts, and distinguish a branded report from a fully branded client application. They are different purchases.

### Planable: consider it when approval must block publishing

[Planable](https://planable.io/pricing/) distinguishes optional approval on Basic from required approval on Pro. Pro lists unlimited users, ten social pages per workspace and 150 posts per workspace per month. Analytics is an additional workspace-level charge.

That is useful when your buying requirement is explicit: nobody publishes until an approver signs off. Test what happens when someone edits an approved post, and whether your process needs one approver or several sequential reviewers. Include reporting and every client workspace in the budget.

## Which alternatives suit inbox work, simple publishing or analytics?

### Agorapulse: shortlist it for community management

[Agorapulse Standard](https://www.agorapulse.com/pricing/) lists an inbox for messages and organic comments, publishing and basic branded reports. Professional adds post and inbox assignments; Advanced adds moderation rules and more extensive reporting.

Standard is $99 per user per month on monthly billing, or $79 billed annually, with ten social profiles listed. Three users therefore start at $297 per month on monthly billing before any additional profiles or upgrades. It can be worth that cost when handling conversations is a paid client service; it is less compelling if you only need a calendar.

### Buffer: shortlist it for a manageable channel count

[Buffer Team](https://buffer.com/pricing) includes unlimited team members, access levels and approval workflows. Its published one-channel annual rate is $10 per month, billed as $120 per year. Essentials is a single-user plan, so its lower entry price is not the right comparison for a collaborating agency.

Use Buffer's calculator for your complete channel count and applicable volume pricing. A channel-priced tool can fit a lean client roster, but budget for every connected client profile. Check the actual report your client expects during the trial.

### Metricool: shortlist it when reports drive the purchase

[Metricool](https://metricool.com/pricing/) organizes plans around brands. Starter lists PDF and PowerPoint reports; Advanced adds team and client management, roles, post approvals and customizable report templates.

Compare Advanced if reviewers need access. Also check the X add-on and Advanced Analytics add-on, rather than treating a headline subscription as the entire cost. Select USD and your billing term before recording a price: the public pricing page can display another currency.

## What would a five-client agency actually need?

Use a concrete workload before comparing quotes. Suppose you have **five clients, three social accounts each and three staff members**. That is 15 connected accounts, three staff seats and five separate client spaces if you require one per client.

Now add the requirements that can change the answer:

1. **Client reviewers:** count them separately from staff and ask whether they consume paid seats.
2. **Approval rules:** decide whether sign-off is a recorded conversation or a publishing restriction enforced by the tool.
3. **Reports:** request one sample with the exact metrics, branding and delivery format you sell.
4. **Networks and formats:** test the actual account types, videos, carousels and first comments your clients need.
5. **Growth:** obtain a second quote for ten clients, plus any network, reporting or workspace add-ons.

On capacity alone, Postbase Pro covers this five-client example at $59 per month, but its network and approval limits still determine suitability. SocialPilot Premium covers the account and staff counts at $100 per month on monthly billing and includes client approvals. Those are different workflows, not an equivalent-feature price comparison.

For the other tools, request the same 15-account, three-staff, five-client configuration. An entry price for one user, one brand or one channel does not answer that question.

## When should an agency stay with Hootsuite?

Stay if the existing setup earns its cost through client services you would otherwise rebuild. [Hootsuite's current plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans) include publishing, inbox, reporting and listening capabilities, with more extensive automation and collaboration on higher tiers.

List the features used in your last three client deliverables. If they include inbox operations, listening reports or complex approvals, compare the exact replacements and migration effort. If the team only opens the calendar to schedule approved posts, a narrower product deserves a trial.

## How do you switch without disrupting client work?

Choose one client as the pilot and define a cutover date. Keep a written record of every queued post, media asset, destination and time zone.

1. Export the reports and historical records you need before ending the old subscription.
2. Connect authorized accounts in the new tool and test permissions with each role.
3. Recreate a small batch as drafts; check formatting and media against the originals.
4. Run one approval cycle and produce one client report.
5. Cancel the old scheduled copies before enabling the replacement schedule, then verify the queue.
6. Confirm successful delivery and assign someone to handle failures before moving the remaining clients.

Do not assume historical analytics, approval records or scheduled posts transfer automatically. Ask each vendor what imports are supported.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best Hootsuite alternative for a small agency?

Start with the work you sell. SocialPilot is a useful shortlist for client approval and reporting, Agorapulse for inbox operations, and Postbase for publishing when its supported networks and manual review process fit. Trial your most demanding client workflow before choosing.

### Which alternative has the lowest agency cost?

There is no universal cheapest option. Compare the plan that covers all client accounts, collaborators, reviewers and required features. A low single-user or single-channel price can miss the tier an agency actually needs.

### Does Postbase include client approval workflows?

Not yet. Postbase's [agency FAQ](/for/agencies) describes reviewing drafts and scheduling them after approval. If approval must be enforced inside the publishing tool, evaluate an alternative with that control.

### Can an AI assistant replace client approval?

No. Drafting or scheduling assistance does not establish that a client approved the final content. Keep a named human reviewer and a clear publishing rule, whether the work starts in a chat or a calendar.

### What should I test before canceling Hootsuite?

Test a complete client cycle: draft, revision, approval, publication, comment handling if required, and the monthly report. Check billing terms and the next renewal date separately from the product trial.
