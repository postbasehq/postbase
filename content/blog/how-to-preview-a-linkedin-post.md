**LinkedIn doesn't have a preview button, so the easiest way to preview a LinkedIn post is to paste it into a free post preview tool, which shows where LinkedIn will cut it off with "...more", how line breaks and formatting look, and how your images will be laid out.** To check a link's preview card, use LinkedIn's own Post Inspector. Then post or schedule it with confidence.

Here's what to check, and how, before a post goes live. Written on 10 November 2026.

## What to check before you publish a LinkedIn post

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Where "...more" falls | LinkedIn shows the first few lines, roughly 200 characters on desktop and less on a phone. Your hook has to land before it |
| Line breaks and spacing | Blank lines and single-line paragraphs change how long the visible part is |
| Bold and italic text | LinkedIn has no formatting buttons for posts; styled text is made of special Unicode characters that look different on some devices |
| Length | Posts can be up to 3,000 characters |
| Images | Several images are arranged in a grid and cropped. Your key detail may be cut off |
| Link preview | The card's title and image come from the linked page, not from your post |
| Mentions and hashtags | They only link if LinkedIn recognises them as you type |

## Option 2: check the link card with Post Inspector

If your post includes a link, LinkedIn builds a preview card from the page's Open Graph tags. Paste the URL into [LinkedIn Post Inspector](https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/) to see the card's title, description and image before you share it. If they're wrong or out of date, our guide to [LinkedIn Post Inspector](/blog/linkedin-post-inspector) explains how to fix and refresh them.

## Option 3: preview it in LinkedIn itself

LinkedIn's post box shows your text as you type it, but not how it will look in the feed. Two workarounds:

- **Schedule it and read it back.** Schedule the post a day ahead, then open it from the clock icon under **View all scheduled posts**. Reading it again later, out of the editor, is a good final check, and you can still edit it. Here's [how to see and edit scheduled posts on LinkedIn](/blog/how-to-see-scheduled-posts-on-linkedin).
- **Check on your phone.** Most LinkedIn reading happens on phones, where fewer lines show before "...more". If you write on a computer, read the scheduled post in the app before it goes out.

LinkedIn has no "only me" visibility for posts, so you can't publish privately to see the result.

## How to write a LinkedIn post that previews well

- **Put the point in the first line.** Not "I've been thinking about…" but the conclusion, the number or the surprise.
- **Keep the first two lines short.** One-line paragraphs at the top make more of your hook visible before the fold.
- **Use formatting sparingly.** A few bold words help; whole bold paragraphs are hard to read and some screen readers read styled letters one at a time.
- **Size images for the feed.** LinkedIn recommends images 1080 pixels wide, in any ratio from 3:1 to 4:5; a 1080 × 1350 portrait image takes up the most room in the feed. Our guide to [LinkedIn image sizes](/blog/linkedin-image-sizes) has every size.
- **Decide where the link goes.** In the post, it creates a preview card; many people put it in the first comment instead. Our guide to the [link in the first comment](/blog/linkedin-link-in-first-comment) weighs it up.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you preview a LinkedIn post before posting?

Not with a preview button in LinkedIn. Use a post preview tool to see how it will look in the feed, or schedule the post and read it back from your scheduled posts before it goes out.

### Where does LinkedIn cut off a post?

After the first few lines, roughly 200 characters on desktop and fewer on a phone, LinkedIn shows "...more". Everything important should be before that point.

### How do I see how my LinkedIn link preview will look?

Paste the URL into LinkedIn Post Inspector at linkedin.com/post-inspector. It shows the title, description and image LinkedIn will use, and refreshes them if the page has changed.

### How do I preview a LinkedIn post on mobile?

Schedule the post, then open it from your scheduled posts in the LinkedIn app, or use a preview tool that shows the mobile fold. Phones show fewer lines before "...more" than desktop.

### Can I post on LinkedIn privately to test it?

No. LinkedIn posts can be public or limited to connections, but there's no "only me" option. Use a preview tool or a scheduled post instead.

### Does LinkedIn have a post preview generator?

LinkedIn doesn't, but free tools do. Our post preview tool shows LinkedIn alongside X, Bluesky, Instagram and other networks, without uploading anything.
