TikTok has a built-in scheduler, but it's easy to miss because it isn't in the phone app. This guide covers how to use it, who can, its limits, and when a scheduling tool makes more sense.

Everything here was checked on 6 October 2026.

## Schedule a TikTok from your computer

1. Go to **tiktok.com** in a desktop browser, sign in and open **TikTok Studio** (the Upload button takes you there).
2. Choose your video.
3. Write the caption, pick a cover and choose who can watch.
4. Switch on **Schedule** and pick a date and time.
5. Click **Schedule**.

TikTok lets you schedule **anywhere from 15 minutes to 10 days** ahead.

## Who can schedule on TikTok

Scheduling is for **Creator and Business accounts**. If you don't see the Schedule switch, check your account type in TikTok's settings; personal accounts may not have it. It's also **desktop only**: the phone app has drafts, but no scheduler.

## The limits of TikTok's scheduler

- **Desktop only.** The scheduler lives in TikTok Studio on the web.
- **10 days maximum.** Planning a month of content means coming back every week or so.
- **Videos only.** Photo carousels, playlists and LIVEs can't be scheduled natively.
- **No editing.** You can't edit a scheduled video. To change the caption or time, delete it and upload it again.
- **TikTok only.** If the same video is going to YouTube Shorts, that's a separate upload in YouTube Studio.

## TikTok's limits at a glance

| What | Limit |
| --- | --- |

## Photo posts

TikTok also supports photo posts, a swipeable carousel of images with music. TikTok's own scheduler can't schedule them, but Postbase can: add up to 35 images instead of a video.

Vertical videos usually work on both. In Postbase, add the video once, pick both channels, and write a caption for each: TikTok's caption is the whole description, while YouTube needs a title (from the first line) and a description. ## Common problems and fixes

**There's no Schedule switch.** You're on a personal account or in the phone app. Switch to a Creator or Business account, and use TikTok Studio in a desktop browser.

**The date picker stops at 10 days.** That's TikTok's limit. Schedule the rest later, or use a tool that holds the video and posts it when the time comes.

**I need to change a scheduled video's caption.** TikTok can't edit a scheduled video. Delete it and schedule it again, or, in Postbase, edit it any time before it goes out.

**A tool's TikTok post came out private.** Every TikTok app starts limited to private posts until TikTok approves it for public posting. Change the video's visibility in the TikTok app, and check whether your tool has been approved.

## Tips for scheduled TikToks

- **Keep the first second strong.** Scheduling doesn't change the algorithm, but posting consistently does help.
- **Write the caption for search.** People search TikTok like a search engine, and captions can be up to 2,200 characters.
- **Label paid partnerships.** TikTok requires it, and a scheduler should make it easy.
- **Batch your week.** Film several videos in one sitting and schedule them across the week.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you schedule TikTok videos on your phone?

Not with TikTok's own scheduler, which only works in a desktop browser through TikTok Studio. The phone app can save drafts but not schedule them.

### How far in advance can you schedule a TikTok?

With TikTok's scheduler, from 15 minutes to 10 days ahead. A scheduling tool can hold a video for longer and post it at the time you choose.

### Do you need a Business account to schedule on TikTok?

You need a Creator or Business account. Personal accounts may not see the Schedule option.

### Can you schedule TikTok photo posts?

Not with TikTok's built-in scheduler, which handles videos only. Tools that use TikTok's Content Posting API, like Postbase, can schedule photo posts.

### Can you edit a scheduled TikTok?

Not in TikTok. You have to delete the scheduled video and upload it again. In a scheduling tool you can usually edit a post until it goes out.

### Do scheduled TikToks get less reach?

TikTok hasn't said that scheduled posts are treated differently. Consistency, the first second and the caption matter far more than how the video was posted.
