**To see your scheduled posts on LinkedIn, click "Start a post", then the clock icon at the bottom of the post box, then "View all scheduled posts".** Each post has a three-dot menu where you can edit it, change its time (Modify schedule) or delete it. In the app, tap the plus button, then Post, then the clock icon. Company page posts are kept separately, under the page's admin view.

LinkedIn hides scheduled posts well, so here's the exact path on each device, checked on 10 November 2026.

## Find scheduled posts on desktop

1. On your LinkedIn home feed, click **Start a post**.
2. In the post box, click the **clock icon** at the bottom.
3. Click **View all scheduled posts**.

You'll see every post scheduled for your profile, in date order, with the date and time each will go out.

## Find scheduled posts in the LinkedIn app

1. Tap the **plus (Post)** button at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap the **clock icon** in the post editor.
3. Tap **View all**.

The app shows the same list as desktop. Labels move around between app versions; if you can't see the clock, start typing a post and it appears in the toolbar.

## Edit a scheduled post

1. Open your scheduled posts (above).
2. Click the **three dots** on the post you want to change.
3. Choose **Edit post**.
4. Change the text, images or formatting.
5. Click **Schedule** to save.

Close the editor without clicking Schedule and your changes are lost; there's no autosave.

## Change the date or time

From the same three-dot menu, choose **Modify schedule**, pick a new date and time, and click **Schedule**. The content stays the same. You can reschedule as often as you like, as long as the new time is within LinkedIn's three-month window.

## Delete or cancel a scheduled post

Choose **Delete** from the three-dot menu and confirm. There's no undo and no draft is kept, so copy the text somewhere first if you might want it later.

To post it now instead of later, the simplest route is to copy the text, delete the scheduled post and publish a new one.

## Scheduled posts for a LinkedIn company page

Page posts don't appear in your personal list, and personal posts don't appear in the page's list.

1. Go to your **Page admin view**.
2. Open **Page posts** and click **Start a post**.
3. Click the **clock icon**, then **View all scheduled posts**.

Edit, reschedule and delete work the same way. Other admins of the page can see and manage posts you schedule for it, which helps when a team shares a page.

## Why can't I see my scheduled posts?

**You're looking in the wrong place.** Scheduled posts aren't on your profile or in your activity until they go out. Only the clock icon in the post box shows them.

**It's a page post.** Check the page's admin view, not your personal post box.

**It has already gone out.** Once published, it moves to your activity like any other post.

**It was scheduled in another tool.** Posts scheduled in a third-party scheduler stay in that tool until they publish. LinkedIn doesn't know about them yet.

## The limits of LinkedIn's scheduler

LinkedIn's built-in scheduler is free and works well for a few posts. It gets awkward as you post more:

- **A list, not a calendar.** You can't see your week or month at a glance, or spot gaps.
- **Three months ahead at most.**
- **LinkedIn only.** If the same idea goes to X or Bluesky, you schedule it again in each app.
- **No first comment.** If you put links in the first comment, you still have to post it yourself after the post goes out.
- **One person's view.** Personal posts can't be shared with a teammate to review.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I find scheduled posts on LinkedIn?

Click Start a post, then the clock icon in the post box, then View all scheduled posts. In the app, tap the plus button, then the clock icon, then View all.

### How do I edit a scheduled post on LinkedIn?

Open your scheduled posts, click the three dots on the post, choose Edit post, make your changes and click Schedule to save them.

### Can you reschedule a post on LinkedIn?

Yes. In your scheduled posts, click the three dots on the post, choose Modify schedule, and pick a new date and time up to three months ahead.

### How do I cancel a scheduled post on LinkedIn?

Click the three dots on the post in your scheduled posts list and choose Delete. Deleting can't be undone, so copy the text first if you need it.

### Can other people see my scheduled LinkedIn posts?

No. Scheduled posts on your profile are visible only to you until they're published. Page posts are visible to the page's other admins.

### How far in advance can you schedule a post on LinkedIn?

Up to three months ahead with LinkedIn's built-in scheduler.

### Can I schedule LinkedIn posts from my phone?

Yes. The LinkedIn app has the same clock icon in the post editor for scheduling and for viewing scheduled posts.
