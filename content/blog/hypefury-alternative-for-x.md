Hypefury built its name on X: threads, auto-plugs, evergreen tweets. So it's a surprise to find this on its pricing page today: "Hypefury no longer supports 𝕏". If you relied on it for X, here's what you can do.

We checked Hypefury's pricing page and homepage on 27 September 2026. Its homepage copy still mentions X in places, so check your own account to see what's changed for you.

## What Hypefury still does

Hypefury still supports Bluesky, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Mastodon, with its AI writing, auto-plugs and auto-DMs. If you've moved off X, it may still suit you. The problem is only if X is where your audience is.

## Your options for X

1. **X's own scheduler.** Free and built into x.com. It's fine for occasional single posts, but it's not built for scheduling whole threads, and it only posts to X. See [how to schedule tweets](/blog/how-to-schedule-tweets).
2. **Another scheduler.** Pick one that supports X threads, and ideally the other networks you already use with Hypefury, so everything's in one place.
3. **The X API.** If you're technical, a script can post for you. X's API is now pay-per-use, so it costs a little per post. We explain it in [How to post to X with the API](/blog/x-api-post-tweet).

## What to look for in a replacement

- **Real thread support.** Posts published as a chain of replies, in order, with media.
- **The other networks you use.** Most Hypefury users also post to Bluesky, LinkedIn or Threads.
- **A calendar,** so you can see the week at a glance.
- **Honest pricing.** Per-channel pricing adds up quickly across five networks.

## Moving your queue without posting twice

1. **Export or copy your queue.** Open each scheduled post in Hypefury and copy the text somewhere safe.
2. **Connect your accounts** in the new tool.
3. **Recreate the posts** with their times. If you use Postbase, you can paste a list into Claude and ask it to recreate them over MCP.
4. **Delete them from Hypefury** for any network it still posts to, so nothing goes out twice.
5. **Check the first week closely** in the new calendar.
