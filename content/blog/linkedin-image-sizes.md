**The best LinkedIn post image size is 1080 pixels wide; LinkedIn accepts any ratio from 3:1 (wide) to 4:5 (portrait), and a 1080 × 1350 portrait image takes up the most space in the feed.** Images must be at least 552 × 276 pixels and under 5 MB, and you can add up to 20 in one post. Link previews use 1200 × 627, your profile banner is 1584 × 396, and a company page cover is 1512 × 256.

Below is every size, from LinkedIn's help pages, with notes on what gets cropped. Checked on 10 November 2026.

## LinkedIn post image size

LinkedIn recommends images **1080 pixels wide**. The height is up to you, within a ratio of 3:1 (very wide) to 4:5 (portrait). Which to pick:

- **Portrait, 1080 × 1350 (4:5):** the most screen space in the feed, especially on phones. Best for charts, quotes and photos of people.
- **Square, 1080 × 1080 (1:1):** looks the same everywhere and reuses well on Instagram and other networks.
- **Landscape, 1200 × 627 (1.91:1):** takes up the least space. Use it for screenshots and wide photos.

Images narrower than 552 × 276 are refused, and files over 5 MB won't upload. JPG or PNG are the safest formats.

## Multi-image posts

You can add up to **20 images** to one post. LinkedIn arranges them in a grid in the feed (the first few, with a "+" count for the rest) and shows them at up to 4:5. Put your most important image first, keep all images the same ratio, and keep text away from the edges, because grid tiles are cropped.

## LinkedIn carousel (document post) size

LinkedIn's native "carousel" today is a **document post**: you upload a PDF (or PowerPoint or Word file) and readers swipe through its pages. Documents can be up to 300 pages.

- **Page size:** design each page at **1080 × 1350** (4:5) or **1080 × 1080** (1:1). These aren't LinkedIn requirements, but they fill the feed and stay readable on phones.
- **Text size:** at least about 40 pixels on a 1080-wide page, or it's unreadable on a phone.
- **Export:** a PDF with all pages the same size. Keep it to the pages that earn a swipe; 8 to 12 is plenty for most.
- **Title:** LinkedIn asks for a document title, shown at the top of the carousel.

## LinkedIn link preview image size

When you share a link, LinkedIn builds a card from the page's `og:image`. LinkedIn asks for **1200 × 627 pixels (1.91:1), up to 5 MB**. If the image is wrong or missing, the fix is on the web page, not in LinkedIn: see our guide to [LinkedIn Post Inspector](/blog/linkedin-post-inspector).

## LinkedIn banner size (profile background)

Your profile background is **1584 × 396 pixels (4:1)**, under 8 MB. The catch is your profile photo, which sits over the lower-left corner, and phones crop the banner further.

Keep text and logos in the **right two-thirds**, away from the lower-left. Download our [LinkedIn banner template (1584 × 396 PNG)](/templates/linkedin-banner-template-1584x396.png), put it on the top layer in your editor, and hide it before you export.

## LinkedIn company page image sizes

From LinkedIn's Page help:

- **Logo:** 400 × 400 recommended, at least 268 × 268. Transparent PNGs are shown on white, so check your logo works on a white background.
- **Cover image:** 1512 × 256. LinkedIn advises keeping key details away from the edges, especially the lower-right corner, which may be cropped on different screens.
- **Life tab main image:** 1128 × 376 (needs Career Pages).
- **Life tab company photos:** 900 × 600 recommended, at least 264 × 176.
- **All page images:** PNG or JPEG, up to 3 MB. A high-resolution JPEG usually looks sharper than a PNG.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best image size for a LinkedIn post?

1080 pixels wide. A 1080 × 1350 portrait (4:5) image takes up the most space in the feed; 1080 × 1080 square is a safe all-rounder. LinkedIn accepts any ratio from 3:1 to 4:5.

### What is the LinkedIn banner size?

1584 × 396 pixels for a personal profile background, under 8 MB. Company page covers are 1512 × 256 pixels, up to 3 MB.

### What size should a LinkedIn carousel be?

Design each page of the PDF at 1080 × 1350 (4:5) or 1080 × 1080 (1:1). Documents can be up to 300 pages, but 8 to 12 pages is usually enough.

### What is the LinkedIn profile picture size?

400 × 400 pixels, square. LinkedIn shows it as a circle, so keep your face in the centre.

### How many images can I post on LinkedIn?

Up to 20 images in one post. LinkedIn shows the first few in a grid and the rest behind a "+" count.

### Why is my LinkedIn image cropped?

It's outside LinkedIn's 3:1 to 4:5 range, it's part of a multi-image grid, or it's a banner hidden behind your profile photo. Use 1080 pixels wide within the ratio range and keep important details away from the edges.

### What is the LinkedIn link preview image size?

1200 × 627 pixels (1.91:1), set as the `og:image` on the page you're linking to, up to 5 MB.
