**Metricool has a free plan for one brand, then Starter from $25 a month (or $20 a month billed yearly) for up to 5 brands, and Advanced from $67 a month ($53 yearly) for up to 15 brands.** A brand includes one profile on each network, which makes Metricool good value for multi-network brands. The costs to watch are add-ons: connecting X costs $10 a month per account, and advanced analytics and automation are extra.

Here's every plan, what each includes, the add-ons and worked examples, checked on Metricool's pricing page on 10 November 2026. Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT.

## What counts as a "brand" in Metricool

This is the key to Metricool's pricing. A **brand** is one business, client or project, and each brand can connect **one profile per network**: one Instagram, one Facebook page, one TikTok, one LinkedIn, one YouTube channel and so on.

So 5 brands isn't 5 social accounts; it can be 5 brands with several networks each. That makes Metricool cheaper than tools priced per channel when each brand uses many networks, and less of a bargain when you have lots of accounts on a single network.

## What each plan includes

**Free**

- 1 brand.
- 20 scheduled posts a month.
- 30 days of analytics.
- 5 competitor profiles.
- 5 AI credits a month.
- Metricool's MCP server for AI tools.
- **No LinkedIn and no X.**

**Starter** (from $25/month)

- Up to 5 or 10 brands, with LinkedIn included.
- Unlimited scheduling under a fair use policy.
- Up to 100 competitors.
- PDF and PowerPoint reports, link in bio, Canva and Google Drive integrations.
- 20 AI credits per brand per month.
- X needs the paid X add-on.

**Advanced** (from $67/month)

- Up to 15, 25 or 50 brands.
- Team and client management, roles and a **post approval** workflow.
- Custom report templates and a Looker Studio connector.
- The **Metricool API**, plus Zapier and Make.
- 35 AI credits per brand per month.

**Custom**

- Any number of brands, white label and a dedicated account manager.

## The add-ons that cost extra

| Add-on | Price | Needed for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X (Twitter) | $10 per month per connected X account | Posting to X and X analytics on paid plans |
| Advanced Analytics | From $12 a month on Starter, from $36 on Advanced | Metricool Studio and unlimited campaign dashboards |
| Flows | $12 a month for 2,500 executions, then $0.01 each | Automations |
| White label, Canva extras | Contact Metricool | Agency branding |

**The X add-on is the one that surprises people.** It's per X account, not per plan, so an agency posting to 10 clients' X accounts pays $100 a month on top of its plan. Buffer, Hootsuite and Postbase include X in the plan price.

## What Metricool actually costs: three examples

**A solo creator** posting to Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and X, one brand:
Starter for up to 5 brands, $25 a month, plus the X add-on, $10. **$35 a month**, or $30 billed yearly.

**A small agency** with 8 clients, each on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, no X:
Starter for up to 10 brands. **$45 a month**, or $36 billed yearly. Very good value. If the agency needs client approvals, it moves to Advanced: **$67 a month**.

**The same agency, with each client also on X:**
Advanced for approvals ($67) plus 8 X add-ons ($80). **$147 a month**.

## Is Metricool worth it?

**It's a good deal if** you manage several brands that each use many networks, you want analytics and competitor tracking as much as scheduling, and X isn't central to what you do.

**It gets expensive if** you post to many X accounts, you need approvals (Advanced only), or you want the API (Advanced only).

**It's limited if** you're on the free plan and use LinkedIn or X, because neither can be connected.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Metricool cost?

Metricool is free for one brand. Paid plans start at $25 a month for up to 5 brands (Starter), or $20 a month billed yearly. Advanced starts at $67 a month for up to 15 brands. X costs an extra $10 a month per account.

### Is Metricool free?

Yes, for one brand with 20 scheduled posts a month and 30 days of analytics. The free plan can't connect LinkedIn or X.

### Does Metricool charge extra for X (Twitter)?

Yes. On paid plans, connecting X needs the X add-on at $10 a month for each X account.

### What is a brand in Metricool?

A business, client or project. Each brand can connect one profile on each social network, so one brand can cover Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and more.

### Does Metricool have an API?

Yes, on the Advanced plan and above, along with Zapier and Make integrations. The free plan includes Metricool's MCP server for AI tools.

### Can I cancel Metricool at any time?

Yes. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, then the account moves to the free plan.

### Does Metricool have post approvals?

Yes, on the Advanced plan, which adds team and client management and an approval workflow.
