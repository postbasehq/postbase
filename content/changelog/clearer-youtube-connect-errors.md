Google lets you untick individual permissions when you connect. If a permission Postbase needs is missing, you cancelled, your account has no YouTube channel, or YouTube's quota is reached, the Channels page now says which one, so you know what to do next.

The connect screen also lists exactly what Postbase can do with your channel.
