Add `https://mcp.postbase.so/mcp` to your AI tool, sign in, and pick the workspace it may post to. There's no key to copy or store.

Your AI can list your channels, save drafts, schedule posts and threads, and cancel scheduled posts. See [the MCP server](/mcp) for the details.
