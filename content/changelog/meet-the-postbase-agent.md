The agent is a chat inside Postbase. Ask it for a post, a thread or a week of updates, and it drafts them using your real connected channels.

Nothing goes out on its own: every post appears as a proposal you can edit, add to drafts or schedule. You can mention a post with `/` or a channel with `@` to give it context, and attach images.
