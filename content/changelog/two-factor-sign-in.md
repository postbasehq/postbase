summary: Protect your account with an authenticator app, and add a backup device so a lost phone never locks you out.

Turn it on in **Settings → Security**. After you sign in with Google, GitHub or your email link, Postbase asks for a 6-digit code from your authenticator app (1Password, Google Authenticator, Authy and others).

Add a second device as a backup. Sensitive actions, like deleting a workspace, ask for a code again.
