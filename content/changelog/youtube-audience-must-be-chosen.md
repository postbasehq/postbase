title: Choose your YouTube audience before scheduling
date: 2026-10-06
type: improved
area: Publishing
summary: Postbase no longer assumes a video isn't made for kids; you choose before it's scheduled.
draft: true
---

The YouTube settings in the composer used to answer "made for kids" with No by default. The question now starts unanswered, and the YouTube tab is flagged until you pick an answer. Posts you'd already saved keep the answer they had.

The title hint also now matches the YouTube channel's own caption when you've written one.
