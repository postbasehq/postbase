Billing for Postbase Cloud runs on Stripe.

## Upgrading

Open **Billing** in the app to pick a [plan](/cloud/plans) and choose monthly or
annual billing. You're taken to Stripe Checkout to enter payment details.

## Managing your subscription

From **Billing** you can change plans, update your card, or cancel through the
Stripe customer portal. Cancelling keeps access until the end of the period.

<Note>
  Trials count as active access. If you reach a channel, seat, or AI limit,
  upgrade your plan to raise it.
</Note>
