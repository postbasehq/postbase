Postbase Cloud is the hosted version at [postbase.so](https://www.postbase.so).
Plans scale by channels, seats, and AI allowances. Posting is unlimited on every
plan (subject to fair use).

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Channels | Seats | AI images + videos / month | Agent messages / month |
| ---- | ----- | -------- | ----- | -------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Creator** | $29/mo | 5 | 1 | 20 + 3 | 150 |
| **Team** | $39/mo | 15 | 5 | 100 + 10 | 500 |
| **Pro** | $59/mo | 50 | 15 | 300 + 30 | 1,500 |

Yearly billing is 10× the monthly price (two months free). Prices are in US
dollars and include any sales tax or VAT.

Every plan includes unlimited posts, all platforms, threads, media & calendar,
the MCP server, and analytics.

<Note>
  A **trial** gives you Creator-level access (5 channels, 1 seat) with a smaller
  AI quota (5 images, 1 video and 25 agent messages) so you can try everything
  before paying.
</Note>

## Limits

- **Channels** — the number of connected accounts, per the table above.
- **Seats** — org members (including pending invites): 1 on Creator, 5 on Team,
  15 on Pro.
- **AI generations** — a monthly quota for [image and video generation](/ai/generate);
  images and videos are counted separately.
- **Agent messages** — each message you send the Postbase agent in the app counts
  as one, however many steps it takes.

When you hit a limit, Postbase points you to upgrade in [Billing](/cloud/billing).
