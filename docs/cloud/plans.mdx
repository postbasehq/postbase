Postbase Cloud is the hosted version at [postbase.so](https://www.postbase.so).
Plans scale by workspaces, channels, seats, and AI allowances. Posting is unlimited
on every plan, subject to [fair use](https://www.postbase.so/terms).

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Workspaces | Channels | Seats | AI images + videos / month | AI agent |
| ---- | ----- | ---------- | -------- | ----- | -------------------------- | -------- |
| **Creator** | $29/mo | 1 | 5 | 1 | 20 + 3 | Unlimited |
| **Team** | $39/mo | 3 | 15 | 5 | 100 + 10 | Unlimited |
| **Pro** | $59/mo | 5 | 50 | 15 | 300 + 30 | Unlimited |
| **Agency** | $99/mo | 20 | 100 | 30 | 600 + 60 | Unlimited |

Yearly billing is 10× the monthly price (two months free). Prices are in US
dollars and include any sales tax or VAT.

Every plan includes unlimited posts, the unlimited AI agent, all platforms,
threads, media & calendar, the MCP server, and analytics.

<Note>
  A **trial** gives you Creator-level access (5 channels, 1 seat) with a smaller
  AI quota (5 images and 1 video) so you can try everything before paying.
</Note>

## Limits

Every limit is shared across the workspaces on your plan.

- **Workspaces** — separate spaces for clients or brands, each with its own channels,
  calendar, media and team. Create one from the workspace menu in the sidebar; it
  shares the plan of the workspace you created it from.
- **Channels** — the number of connected accounts across your workspaces, per the
  table above.
- **Seats** — people across your workspaces, including pending invites. Someone in
  several of your workspaces counts once: 1 on Creator, 5 on Team, 15 on Pro and 30
  on Agency.
- **AI generations** — a monthly quota for [image and video generation](/ai/generate);
  images and videos are counted separately.
- **AI agent** — unlimited under fair use. A daily safety limit per workspace (well
  above normal use, lower during the trial) stops scripts or runaway sessions; it
  resets at midnight UTC. The usage ring in the agent chat shows your AI image and
  video allowances.

When you hit a limit, Postbase points you to upgrade in [Billing](/cloud/billing).
