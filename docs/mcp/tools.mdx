Once connected, your agent can call these tools. Everything is scoped to the
authorized workspace.

## `list_channels`

List the connected social accounts and their platforms. Use the returned
channel ids with `create_post`.

## `create_post`

Draft or schedule a post or thread.

| Argument | Type | Notes |
| -------- | ---- | ----- |
| `body` | string | The post text (single post). |
| `thread` | string[] | Multiple posts to publish as a thread. |
| `channel_ids` | string[] | Channels to publish to (from `list_channels`). |
| `scheduled_at` | string | ISO 8601 time to publish, with a timezone (`Z` or an offset like `+01:00`). Omit for a draft. |
| `schedule_in_minutes` | number | Publish this many minutes from now, on the server's clock. Used for relative times like "in 10 minutes". |
| `media_ids` | string[] | Files from `list_media` to attach, in order. |
| `youtube` | object | `title` (up to 100 characters), `privacy` (`public`, `unlisted` or `private`) and `made_for_kids` (required to schedule to YouTube). |

Provide `body`, `thread` or `media_ids`. Omit `scheduled_at` and `schedule_in_minutes` to save a draft.

Media follows each network's rules: up to 4 images or one video on X, up to 4
images on Bluesky and Mastodon, up to 20 images on LinkedIn, and one video on
YouTube. A scheduled post that a network would reject is refused with the reason.

<Note>
  TikTok posts can only be saved as drafts from an AI tool. TikTok requires the
  creator to choose who sees each post and agree to its terms, so you schedule
  TikTok drafts yourself in the Postbase composer.
</Note>

## `list_media`

List the images and videos in your Postbase [media library](/using/media),
newest first (up to 50). Filter with `type` (`image` or `video`) or `search`
(part of a file name). Pass the returned ids to `create_post` as `media_ids`.

The agent can't upload files itself: upload them in Postbase first.

## `list_scheduled`

List scheduled/queued posts. Optionally filter by `status` (e.g. `scheduled`
or `draft`).

## `cancel_post`

Cancel a scheduled post by `post_id` — it reverts to a draft.

<Tip>
  You rarely call these by name — just ask in plain language, e.g. "what's
  queued for tomorrow?" or "cancel the LinkedIn post at 5pm."
</Tip>
