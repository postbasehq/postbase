title: Calendar & queue
description: "See everything scheduled, and manage drafts and the publish queue."
---

## Calendar

The calendar shows your scheduled posts by **day**, **week**, or **month**.
Click an empty slot to start a post prefilled with that time, or click a
scheduled post to edit it.

<Tip>
  Opening the week view jumps to where your latest scheduled post sits, so you
  land on the busy part of your week.
</Tip>

## Queue

The **Queue** lists posts waiting to publish and their per-channel status. A
background job publishes each post when it's due — no action needed. If a
channel fails (for example an expired token), it shows there so you can retry or
reconnect.

## Drafts

Unscheduled posts are saved as **drafts**. The Drafts page lists them with a
content preview, the channels they target, and when they were last updated.
Open one to keep editing, or start a fresh post from it in the composer.
